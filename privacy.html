Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026 · Prototype policy; review before public launch.

Information we collect

Depending on the feature used, Kisan Connect may process your name, email address, account role, produce listing details, contact details you choose to publish, buyer enquiries, and price reports you submit. Location names entered for weather searches are sent to the weather provider to return a forecast.

How information is used

Information is used to operate accounts, show listings, handle enquiries, calculate farm estimates, and improve the service. Do not submit Aadhaar numbers, bank details, passwords, or other sensitive information into free-text fields.

Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by hosting/database providers and external services you request, such as weather or configured AI services. Public produce listings and the contact details you choose to publish may be visible to other users. Do not upload another person's photo or personal information without permission.

Security and retention

The backend uses password hashing and HttpOnly session cookies. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Data is retained while needed to provide the service and may remain in backups until those backups expire. Contact the project team to request account/data deletion; an operational process must be configured before public launch.

Your choices

You can avoid optional features, keep personal details out of public listings, and log out after use. Browser speech recognition may be processed by your browser/platform provider. Offline cache stores only app shell assets, not account API responses.

Children and changes

This service is designed for adult users involved in farming or agricultural commerce, not children. This policy may change as the prototype evolves.

Contact

Use the Contact Us page. Replace the project contact details there before public launch.
