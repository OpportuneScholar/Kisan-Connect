Key Seasonal Crop Directives (Rabi 2026-27)

Wheat Sowing Window:

Optimum sowing temperature is 20-22°C. Treat seed with Carboxin + Thiram (2g/kg seed) to prevent loose smut.

Mustard Aphid Caution:

Install yellow sticky traps early. Never spray during morning bee pollination hours (8 AM to 11 AM).

Water Conservation:

For small plots, irrigate at critical stages only (CRI stage at 21 days for wheat). Avoid excess flood irrigation.
